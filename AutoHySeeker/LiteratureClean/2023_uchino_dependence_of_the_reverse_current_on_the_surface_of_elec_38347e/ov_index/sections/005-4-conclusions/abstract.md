Section Role: This section presents the Conclusions of the study on leak and reverse currents in an alkaline water electrolyzer.  
Core Content: Using a bipolar-type alkaline electrolyzer with nickel mesh electrodes, the study found that reverse current is driven primarily by active materials on Ni electrodes and secondarily by H₂/O₂ gas reactions, with electrolyte replacement reducing reverse charge by 10–20%; cell voltages (e.g., U₁, U₂, U₁ᵣ, U₂ᵣ) and redox potentials indicated that reductant–oxidant imbalance shifts bipolar plate potentials toward oxidation, informing strategies to mitigate electrode deterioration.
