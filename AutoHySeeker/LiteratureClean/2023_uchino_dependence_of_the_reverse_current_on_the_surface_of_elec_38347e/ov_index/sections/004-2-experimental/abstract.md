章节定位：Methods  
核心内容：本章节详细描述了碱性水电解实验系统的结构、操作流程及电化学测量方法，并建立了电解槽的等效电路模型用于分析电流分布与电压特性。  
关键实体：Ni meshes, Nafion membranes (NRE212CS), 7.0 M NaOH solution, bipolar-type electrolyzer, finite gap cells, constant current electrolysis, reverse current, cell voltage, AC impedance method, Tafel equation, equivalent circuit, projected electrode area, manifold resistance, electrolyte circulation
