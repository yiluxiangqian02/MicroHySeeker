Paper Positioning: This paper introduces lead-decorated nickel (Pb/Ni) cathode catalysts that simultaneously enhance reverse-current (RC) tolerance and hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) activity in zero-gap alkaline water electrolysis (AWE) systems, addressing a critical durability challenge during startup/shutdown events.

Section Index:
  001-reverse-current-tolerance-for-hydrogen-evolution-reaction-activi: Reverse-Current Tolerance for Hydrogen Evolution Reaction Activity of Lead-Decorated Nickel Catalysts in Zero-Gap Alkaline Water Electrolysis Systems  
  002-1-introduction: 1. Introduction – Outlines the challenge of reverse-current-induced degradation in AWE systems during shutdowns and motivates the need for material-level solutions to improve cathode durability without system-level modifications.  
  003-2-results-and-discussion: 2. Results and Discussion – Presents electrochemical performance data showing that Pb/Ni catalysts not only resist RC-induced degradation but also exhibit improved HER activity after repeated RC cycles, supported by metrics, impedance, and kinetic analysis.  
  004-3-conclusion: 3. Conclusion – Summarizes the dual benefit of Pb decoration on Ni cathodes: conferring RC tolerance through sacrificial oxidation and enhancing HER kinetics via improved proton desorption and water dissociation, validated in real AWE stack testing.

Key Entities: Pb/Ni (lead-decorated nickel) catalysts, reverse-current (RC) simulation via chronopotentiometry and linear sweep voltammetry (LSV), HER activity metrics (RCSF_η, RCAF_η, Tafel slope, charge-transfer resistance), 1 M KOH electrolyte, zero-gap alkaline water electrolysis (AWE) stack, startup/shutdown (SU/SD) protocol, overpotential at −10 mA cm⁻², RHE-referenced potentials, β-Ni(OH)₂/NiO passivation threshold (>0.6 V vs. RHE).
