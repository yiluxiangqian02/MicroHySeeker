Section Role: This section presents results on the degradation tolerance of Pb-Ni electrocatalysts under operational conditions.  
Core Content: The study evaluates the stability and performance decay of Pb-Ni electrocatalysts over 100 hours of continuous operation in 0.1 M KOH at 25°C, reporting a current density retention of 87–92% and a voltage increase of ≤15 mV at 10 mA/cm².  
Key Entities: Pb-Ni electrocatalysts, 0.1 M KOH, 25°C, 100 hours, current density retention (87–92%), voltage increase (≤15 mV), 10 mA/cm²
