Section Role: This section presents experimental results examining the water activation effect on radiation-crosslinked (RC) tolerant Pb-Ni-Ca alloy systems.  
Core Content: The study evaluates the influence of water activation on the mechanical and corrosion resistance properties of Pb-Ni-Ca alloys under radiation-crosslinking conditions, reporting tensile strength retention between 82–94%, elongation at break from 12–18%, and corrosion rates reduced by 30–45% in activated water environments compared to deionized water controls.  
Key Entities: Pb-Ni-Ca alloy, radiation crosslinking (RC), water activation, tensile strength retention (82–94%), elongation at break (12–18%), corrosion rate reduction (30–45%), deionized water
