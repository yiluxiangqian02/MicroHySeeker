Section Role: This section presents the Results of an investigation into ligand effects in Pt–Ni catalysts for oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) under realistic fuel cell conditions.  
Core Content: The study evaluates how varying phosphine ligands—specifically PPh₃, PCy₃, and P(o-Tol)₃—affect the activity and durability of Pt–Ni octahedral nanoparticles, reporting mass activities ranging from 0.45 to 0.82 A mg⁻¹ₚₜ at 0.9 V vs. RHE in 0.1 M HClO₄, with accelerated stress tests showing <15% loss in electrochemical surface area after 30,000 cycles.  
Key Entities: Pt–Ni octahedral nanoparticles, PPh₃, PCy₃, P(o-Tol)₃, oxygen reduction reaction (ORR), 0.1 M HClO₄, 0.9 V vs. RHE, mass activity (0.45–0.82 A mg⁻¹ₚₜ), accelerated stress tests, 30,000 cycles, <15% ECSA loss
