Section Role: This section presents the Results and Discussion of electrocatalyst performance under reverse-current (RC) conditions.  
Core Content: The study evaluates the degradation tolerance and HER activity of Pb/Ni and other M/Ni (M = Sn, Zn, Al, Pb) electrocatalysts under repeated RC cycles in 1 M and 5 M KOH, using metrics RCSF_η and RCAF_η at η = 250 mV, with Pb/Ni showing superior stability (RCSF_η = 6.35) and activity (RCAF_η = 14.11 mA cm⁻²), enhanced kinetics (Tafel slope = 74 mV dec⁻¹, R_ct = 17.3 Ω), and improved performance over 700 RC cycles, attributed to water-dissociation enhancement and ligand-induced proton desorption.
