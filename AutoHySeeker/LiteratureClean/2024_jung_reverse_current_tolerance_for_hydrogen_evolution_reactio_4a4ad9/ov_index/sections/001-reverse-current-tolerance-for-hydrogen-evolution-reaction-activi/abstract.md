Core Content: This section introduces Pb-decorated Ni cathode catalysts (Pb/Ni) as a strategy to enhance reverse-current (RC) tolerance in zero-gap alkaline water electrolysis (AWE) systems, demonstrating that post-RC Pb/Ni catalysts improve hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) activity by promoting proton desorption and water dissociation, with successful AWE stack operation under startup/shutdown (SU/SD) protocols showing enhanced RC tolerance.
