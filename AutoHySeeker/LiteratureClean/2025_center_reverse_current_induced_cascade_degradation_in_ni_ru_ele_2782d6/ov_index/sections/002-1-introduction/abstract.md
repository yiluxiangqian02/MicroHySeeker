Section Role: This section is the Introduction, establishing the research context, problem statement, and objectives regarding reverse current-induced degradation in Ni-Ru electrodes for renewable energy-powered alkaline water electrolysis.  
Core Content: The study investigates RC-induced degradation of Ni-Ru (NR) electrodes under a practical "RC-like" accelerated stress test (AST) with 1-s resolution and variable RC fractions (0–20%), revealing a 214-mV overpotential increase at 400 mA cm⁻², total Ru depletion, Ni substrate damage, and a three-stage degradation mechanism, with degradation rates ranging from 0.257 to 4.756 mV h⁻¹ depending on RC amplitude.  
Key Entities: Ni-Ru electrode (NR), reverse current (RC), alkaline water electrolysis (AWE), renewable energy (RE), accelerated stress test (AST), constant potential test (CP), overpotential (214 mV at 400 mA cm⁻²), RC fraction (0–20%), degradation rate (0.257–4.756 mV h⁻¹), Ostwald ripening, galvanic corrosion, Ru depletion, Ni substrate damage
