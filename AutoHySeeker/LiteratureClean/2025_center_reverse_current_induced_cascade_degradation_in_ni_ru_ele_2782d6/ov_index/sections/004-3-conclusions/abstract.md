Section Role: This section presents the conclusions of the study on RC-induced degradation mechanisms in Ni-Ru (NR) electrodes.  
Core Content: The authors elucidate a cascade degradation process in NR electrodes under reverse cycling (RC) conditions, where initial Ru dissolution at excess reverse potentials triggers surface defects and free Ru ions, leading to Ni substrate corrosion via a Ni-Ru-electrolyte galvanic reaction, with degradation rates dropping from 4.756 to 0.257 mV h⁻¹ as RC fraction decreases from 20% to 0%.  
Key Entities: Ni-Ru (NR) electrodes, reverse cycling (RC), Ru dissolution, Ni substrate corrosion, galvanic reaction, time-resolved electrochemical measurements, morphological analysis, compositional analysis, RC fraction (0%–20%), degradation rate (0.257–4.756 mV h⁻¹), RE-AWE
