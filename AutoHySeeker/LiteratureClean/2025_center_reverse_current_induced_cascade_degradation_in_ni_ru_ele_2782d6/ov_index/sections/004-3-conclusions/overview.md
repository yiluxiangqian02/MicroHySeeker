Paragraph Index:  
P001: Introduces the two-step "RC-like" AST protocol developed to emulate regular-reverse current cycling conditions relevant to real-world electrolyzer operation.  
P002: Describes the multimodal analytical approach—combining time-resolved electrochemistry, morphology, and composition analysis—to reveal the full degradation trajectory of the Ni-Ru (NR) electrode, beginning with Ru dissolution under excessive reverse potential.
