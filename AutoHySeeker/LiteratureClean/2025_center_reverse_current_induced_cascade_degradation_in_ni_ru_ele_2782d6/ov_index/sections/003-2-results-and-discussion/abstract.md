Section Role: This section presents the Results and Discussion of electrochemical degradation in Ni-Ru (NR) cathodes under accelerated stress testing (AST).  
Core Content: The study evaluates NR electrode degradation under a two-step square-wave AST protocol (−1.3 V vs RHE for 2 s and 0.5 V vs RHE for 1 s) over 60,000 cycles, comparing it to constant potential (CP) testing at −1.3 V vs RHE for 50 h; key metrics include overpotentials (η₁₀₀, η₄₀₀, η₈₀₀), degradation rate (mV h⁻¹), ECSA, charge transfer resistance (Rct), ohmic resistance (RΩ), and morphological/elemental changes via SEM, EDS, TEM, XPS, and ICP-MS, revealing a three-stage degradation mechanism involving Ru dissolution, Ni/Ru co-deposition, and final Ru depletion, with degradation rates of 4.756 mV h⁻¹ (AST) vs. 0.473 mV h⁻¹ (CP) and η₄₀₀ increasing from 250 mV (pristine) to 488 mV (AST).  
Key Entities: Ni-Ru (NR) cathode, accelerated stress testing (AST), constant potential (CP), −1.3 V vs RHE, 0.5 V vs RHE, 60,000 cycles, 50 h, overpotential (η₄₀₀ = 250–488 mV), degradation rate (0.473–4.756 mV h⁻¹), ECSA (0.52–0.72), Rct (0.48–2.02 Ω), RΩ (0.87–1.15 Ω), Ru atomic percentage (4.79 to 0 at.%), SEM, EDS, TEM, XPS, ICP-MS, Pourbaix diagram, galvanic corrosion, RC amplitude (−20%, −10%, 0%), reverse potentials (1.43, 0.43, −0.04 V vs SHE)
