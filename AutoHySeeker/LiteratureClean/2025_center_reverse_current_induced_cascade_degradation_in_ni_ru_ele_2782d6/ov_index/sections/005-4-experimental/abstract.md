Section Role: This section describes the experimental methods used to evaluate electrode degradation under regular-reverse potential cycling.  
Core Content: The study employed Ni mesh with in situ deposited Ru (NR electrode) in a 1 M KOH three-electrode system and a zero-gap alkaline electrolyzer, subjecting it to accelerated stress tests (AST) with alternating current densities of 100 mA cm⁻² (2 s) and –20 mA cm⁻² (1 s), while monitoring degradation via cyclic voltammetry (CV), linear sweep voltammetry (LSV), electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), SEM, EDS, XPS, and ICP-OES/MS over up to 60,000 cycles at 80°C.  
Key Entities: Ni mesh with Ru (NR electrode), 1 M KOH, 100 mA cm⁻² / –20 mA cm⁻², 2 s / 1 s cycle duration, three-electrode cell, Hg/HgO reference, Pt counter electrode, ECSA, CV (20–100 mV s⁻¹), LSV (–0.7 to –2.7 V vs. RHE, 5 mV s⁻¹), EIS (100 kHz–0.1 Hz, 10 mV, –1.2 V vs. RHE), RΩ and Rct from ECM, Agfa-500 diaphragm (500 μm), 7 M KOH, 15 mL min⁻¹ flow rate, 80°C, 60,000 cycles, SEM (3 kV), EDS, XPS (Al Kα, C 1s = 284.8 eV), ICP-OES/MS (Agilent 7850), Osterwalder ripening, 500-cycle measurement step
