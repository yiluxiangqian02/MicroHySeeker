Section Role: This section presents the Results and Discussion of electrochemical behaviors and degradation mechanisms in nickel-based anion exchange water electrolyzers (AWEs) under reverse current (RC) conditions simulating startup/shutdown cycles.  
Core Content: Using a custom three-electrode zero-gap AWE cell with Ni felt electrodes, Zirfon separators, 7 m KOH at 80 °C, and a reference electrode positioned 6.5 cm from the cathode edge, the study quantifies polarization losses (anode: 420 mV, cathode: 310 mV at 2 A cm⁻²), induces RC via 0 V shutdown pulses yielding transient −2.4 A cm⁻² currents, and performs 45k-cycle RC accelerated stress testing (AST), revealing anode performance improvement (−250 mV overpotential at 2 A cm⁻² after 7.5k cycles) but severe cathode degradation (+514 mV overpotential at 2 A cm⁻² after 45k cycles), attributed to irreversible β-Ni(OH)₂ formation, increased ohmic loss (136 mV rise), and structural failure.  
Key Entities: Ni felt electrodes, Zirfon separator (Agfa Perl UTP 500), 7 m KOH, 80 °C, 20 ml min⁻¹ electrolyte flow, three-electrode MEA, reference electrode at 6.5 cm, misalignment factor 5.2, 2 A cm⁻², anode overpotential 420 mV, cathode overpotential 310 mV, residual anode overpotential 143 mV, reverse current −2.4 A cm⁻², 0 V shutdown, 45k AST cycles, HFR increase ≈185 mV, anode kinetic overpotential reduction 200 mV, cathode kinetic overpotential increase 350 mV, cathode ohmic overpotential increase 136 mV, β-Ni(OH)₂, NiOOH, Tafel slopes (anode: 102 → 66 mV dec⁻¹; cathode: 81 → 116 mV dec⁻¹), SEM, XRD, Raman, in situ APXPS, XAS
