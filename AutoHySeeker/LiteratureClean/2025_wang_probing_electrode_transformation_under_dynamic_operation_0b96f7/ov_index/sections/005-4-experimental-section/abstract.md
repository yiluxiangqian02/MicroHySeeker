Section Role: This section describes the experimental methods and materials used in the study of alkaline water electrolysis (AWE) cells.  
Core Content: The study employed nickel felt electrodes (Bekaert 2Ni18-0.25), Zirfon UTP 500 separator, ETFE gaskets (10–20 mil), 7 M KOH at 80 °C, and a custom three-electrode setup with Ag/AgCl reference electrode calibrated in pH 14 KOH at 60 °C; electrochemical measurements included polarization curves (20 s per current step), GEIS (1 Hz–700 kHz, 5% current amplitude or 200 mA), ECSA via double-layer capacitance in 0.5 M KOH, and material characterization by SEM/EDS, XRD, HR-TEM, XPS, Raman (532 nm laser, 6 mW), in situ APXPS (4 keV photon energy, 18 Torr), and Ni K-edge XAS.  
Key Entities: Nickel felt electrodes (Bekaert 2Ni18-0.25), Zirfon UTP 500 separator, ETFE gaskets (10–20 mil), 7 M KOH, 80 °C operating temperature, Ag/AgCl reference electrode, 0.5 M KOH, 60 °C calibration, Biologic VSP 300 potentiostat, chronopotentiometry (20 s steps), GEIS (1 Hz–700 kHz, 5% amplitude or 200 mA), HFR, ECSA via Cdl, SEM/EDS (10 kV), XRD (Bragg-Brentano), HR-TEM (200 kV), XPS (Al Kα, 7486.6 eV, 7.5×10⁻⁹ Torr), Raman (532 nm, 6 mW, 400–500 cm⁻¹), APXPS (4 keV, 18 Torr), Ni K-edge XAS
