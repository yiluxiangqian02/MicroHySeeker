Section Role: This section presents the results of an investigation into the degradation mechanism of perovskite solar cells under operational stressors.  
Core Content: The study identifies that methylammonium lead iodide (MAPbI₃) perovskite films degrade significantly under combined light soaking (1 sun, AM1.5G), elevated temperature (85 °C), and ambient humidity (30–50% RH), with power conversion efficiency dropping from 19.2% to 11.4% within 300 hours, primarily due to iodide migration and PbI₂ formation confirmed via XRD and TOF-SIMS.
