Section Role: This section presents the Conclusion of the study.  
Core Content: The work elucidates nickel electrode transformation and degradation mechanisms in alkaline water electrolysis under dynamic startup/shutdown conditions simulated via a RC accelerated stress test (AST) protocol using a three-electrode membrane electrode assembly (MEA) with a reference electrode, revealing anode performance enhancement due to Ni (oxy)hydroxide formation (improving OER kinetics) and cathode degradation from irreversible conversion to β-Ni(OH)₂, causing rising ohmic/kinetic overpotentials (0–1.8 V range inferred from AST), with suggestions for alternative cathode materials like platinum.  
Key Entities: nickel electrodes, alkaline water electrolysis (AWE), three-electrode MEA, reference electrode, RC accelerated stress test (AST), startup/shutdown operations, Ni (oxy)hydroxides, β-Ni(OH)₂, oxygen evolution reaction (OER), hydrogen evolution reaction (HER), ohmic overpotential, kinetic overpotential, platinum, redox reversibility, 0–1.8 V (inferred AST voltage range)
