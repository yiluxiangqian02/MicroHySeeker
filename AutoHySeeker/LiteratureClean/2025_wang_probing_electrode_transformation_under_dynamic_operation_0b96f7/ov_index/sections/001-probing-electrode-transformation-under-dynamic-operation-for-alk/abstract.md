Section Role: This section presents the Introduction and core findings of the study.  
Core Content: Using a three-electrode membrane electrode assembly (MEA) setup and an accelerated stress testing protocol simulating frequent startup/shutdown cycles, the study reveals that dynamic operation induces opposing transformations in nickel-based electrodes: anode performance improves due to continuous formation of nickel (oxy)hydroxides (enhancing oxygen evolution reaction activity), while cathode degrades irreversibly via oxidation to β-nickel hydroxide, causing material dissolution, reduced conductivity, and loss of hydrogen evolution reaction activity.  
Key Entities: alkaline water electrolyzers (AWEs), nickel (oxy)hydroxides, β-nickel hydroxide, three-electrode MEA setup, accelerated stress testing, reverse current, oxygen evolution reaction (OER), hydrogen evolution reaction (HER), operando cell studies, in situ characterization, post-mortem characterization, startup/shutdown cycles
