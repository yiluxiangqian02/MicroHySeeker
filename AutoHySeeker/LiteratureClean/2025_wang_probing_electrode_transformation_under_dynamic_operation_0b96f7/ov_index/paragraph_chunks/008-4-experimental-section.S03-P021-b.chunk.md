Prior to the functional operations, the repeated cycling voltammetry (CV) was carried out to actively age the electrode surface and CV was conducted until two adjacent curves looked similar. Next, open-circuit potential scans were performed before OER. The normal operations for OER were held at 1.6 V versus RHE for 30 min. And the RC operation for OER was held at 0.9 V versus RHE for 30 min. After each operational stage, the electrodes were raised out of the electrolyte by the manipulator for in situ APXPS measurements. The WE was then moved to the focal point of the X-ray beam, $0 . 3 5 ~ \mathsf { m m }$ away from the front cone. The in situ XPS spectra were then collected on the WE with an incident photon energy of 4 keV. Almost all spectra were fitted with Gaussian–Lorentzian peaks after the Shirley background was applied; the sole exception was the Lorentzian Asymmetric shape for the metallic Ni peak, as it showed an obvious asymmetric feature in the Ni 2p region.
