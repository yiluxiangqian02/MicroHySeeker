Section Role: This section serves as the Introduction, establishing the problem of reverse current in water electrolysis and outlining the scope of the review.  
Core Content: It elucidates the electrochemical mechanisms of reverse current—occurring during shutdown or variable renewable energy input—and its detrimental impact on cathode degradation and hydrogen production efficiency, while reviewing mitigation strategies including electrode modification with dopants and protective coatings, system-level controls, multifunctional dynamic protective layers, and in situ/operando characterization techniques.  
Key Entities: reverse current, water electrolysis, cathode degradation, hydrogen production efficiency, electrode modification, dopants, protective coatings, system-level regulation, multifunctional dynamic protective layers, in situ techniques, operando techniques, renewable energy fluctuation, shutdown conditions
