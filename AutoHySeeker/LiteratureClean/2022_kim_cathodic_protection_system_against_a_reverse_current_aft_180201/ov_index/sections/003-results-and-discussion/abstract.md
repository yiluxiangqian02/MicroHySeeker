Section Role: This section presents the Results and Discussion of experimental and analytical investigations into reverse-current-induced degradation in alkaline water electrolyzers and the efficacy of cathodic protection strategies.  
Core Content: The section demonstrates that reverse-current during shut-down causes irreversible oxidation of Ni cathodes to inactive hydroxide/oxide phases, degrading HER performance, and shows that cathodic protection using sacrificial anodes (e.g., Pb, Zn, Al, Sn) effectively suppresses this degradation by maintaining the Ni electrode in a metallic or reversibly oxidized state.
