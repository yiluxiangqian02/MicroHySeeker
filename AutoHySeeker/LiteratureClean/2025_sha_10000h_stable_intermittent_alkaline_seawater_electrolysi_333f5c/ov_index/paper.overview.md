Paper Positioning: This paper identifies and addresses cathode degradation during intermittent alkaline seawater electrolysis driven by renewable energy, proposing a NiCoP–Cr₂O₃ cathode with an in situ-formed phosphate passivation layer that enables 10,000-hour stability under fluctuating operational conditions.
