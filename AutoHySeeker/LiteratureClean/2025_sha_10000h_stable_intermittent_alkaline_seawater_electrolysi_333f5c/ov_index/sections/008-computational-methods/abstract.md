Section Role: This section describes the computational methods used in the study.  
Core Content: Spin-polarized density functional theory (DFT) calculations were performed using VASP with PBE-GGA exchange-correlation functionals, PAW potentials, and Hubbard U corrections (Ni: 6.4, Cr: 3.3, Co: 3.5 eV); structural optimizations employed 400–500 eV plane-wave cutoffs, Monkhorst–Pack k-point grids (e.g., 3×3×3, 5×2×1, 5×4×1), 18 Å vacuum layers, and relaxation criteria of 10⁻⁵ eV and 0.02–0.03 eV/Å; oxygen migration pathways (~2 Å diffusion distances) across CoO/amorphous CoPO₄, amorphous CoPO₄/CoO, and CoO/NiCoP interfaces were analyzed via CI-NEB with four intermediate images per path, while amorphous CoPO₄ (Pna2, 48 atoms) was generated by melt-quenching from 3000 K to 300 K over 20 ps using ab initio molecular dynamics.
