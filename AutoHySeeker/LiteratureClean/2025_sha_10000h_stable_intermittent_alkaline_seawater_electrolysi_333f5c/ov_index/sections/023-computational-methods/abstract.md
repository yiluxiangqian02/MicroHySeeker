章节定位：Methods  
核心内容：本章节详细描述了基于密度泛函理论（DFT）的计算方法，用于研究NiCoP–Cr₂O₃及相关界面体系的电子结构与氧迁移行为。  
关键实体：密度泛函理论（DFT）, Vienna ab initio simulation package (VASP), projector augmented wave potentials, Perdew–Burke–Ernzerhof (PBE) functional, U-values, climbing image nudged elastic band (CI-NEB), plane-wave basis set, k-point sampling, vacuum layer, amorphous CoPO₄/CoO, NiCoP/CoO, melt quenching method, ab initio molecular dynamics, oxygen migration, diffusion paths, force convergence criterion
