Section Role: This section presents the reaction mechanism underlying the stable performance of NiCoP–Cr₂O₃ during intermittent water/seawater electrolysis.  
Core Content: Using TOF-SIMS, Ar⁺ XPS, operando Raman, HAADF-STEM/EELS, and ion chromatography, the study reveals that after a 24-h shutdown, Co segregates to form surface and interior oxide layers, P migrates to create a phosphate-rich sublayer (<10 ppb dissolved PO₄³⁻), and Ni remains predominantly zero-valent (Ni⁰ intensity increases from 10⁻² to 10⁻¹), enabling dynamic passivation and recovery under intermittent HER conditions at −0.1 V vs. RHE with 3,600-s idle periods.
