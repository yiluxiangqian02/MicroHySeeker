Section Role: Results  
Core Content: This section presents the hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) activity and intermittent electrolysis stability of a NiCoP–Cr₂O₃ cathode, demonstrating excellent performance in 1 M NaOH, 1 M NaOH + seawater, and 20 wt% NaOH + seawater (Yellow Sea), with overpotentials as low as 275 mV at 4 A cm⁻² and stable operation for up to 10,000 h under 12-h start–shutdown cycles at 0.5 A cm⁻², outperforming NiCo–LDH which showed 20.58% decay in 800 h.
