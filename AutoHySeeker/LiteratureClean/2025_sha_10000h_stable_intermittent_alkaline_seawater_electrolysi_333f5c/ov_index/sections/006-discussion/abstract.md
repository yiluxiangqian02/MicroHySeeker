Section Role: This section discusses the challenges and mitigation strategies for cathode degradation during intermittent water electrolysis using renewable energy, with a focus on seawater feedstock.  
Core Content: The study demonstrates that in situ formation of a phosphate-based passivation layer using phosphorus and Cr₂O₃ on a NiCoP HER electrocatalyst effectively suppresses halide-induced corrosion and electrode oxidation during shutdown periods, enabling 10,000 hours of stable intermittent seawater electrolysis at 0.5 A cm⁻² with a voltage decay rate below 0.5% per khr.  
Key Entities: NiCoP–Cr₂O₃ HER electrocatalyst, seawater feedstock, intermittent electrolysis, phosphate passivation layer, halide ion corrosion, electrode oxidation, 0.5 A cm⁻², 10,000 h, <0.5% khr⁻¹ voltage decay rate
