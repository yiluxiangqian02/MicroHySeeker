Section Role: This section describes the experimental Methods used to synthesize and characterize a series of electrocatalysts and evaluate their performance in alkaline and seawater electrolysis.  
Core Content: The study details hydrothermal synthesis of NiCo–LDH, NiCoFe hydroxide, and NiFe–LDH on nickel foam, followed by phosphorization to form NiCoP, NiCoFeP, and NiFeP using NaH₂PO₂·H₂O (0.5–0.58 g) at 300 °C for 2 h under Ar; electrodeposition of Cr(OH)₃ onto NiCoP at −10 mA cm⁻² for 10–1,800 s, then calcination at 300 °C to yield NiCoP–Cr₂O₃; preparation of Ni/NiO/Cr₂O₃ via co-electrodeposition and H₂/Ar calcination; and comprehensive characterization (SEM, XRD, XPS, HAADF-STEM, TOF-SIMS, operando Raman/ATR-SEIRAS) alongside electrochemical testing in 1 M KOH or seawater at 60–80 °C, including LSV (10 mV s⁻¹), EIS (5 mV, 0.1 Hz–1 MHz), and stability at 1 A cm⁻².  
Key Entities: NiCo–LDH, NiCoP, NiCoFeP, NiFeP, Ni/NiO/Cr₂O₃, NiCoP–Cr₂O₃, Ni foam (2×3 cm², 3×4 cm²), Ni(NO₃)₂·6H₂O, Co(NO₃)₂·6H₂O, Fe(NO₃)₃·9H₂O, Cr(NO₃)₃·9H₂O, NH₄F, urea, NaH₂PO₂·H₂O (0.5–0.58 g), hydrothermal (100–120 °C, 6–8 h), phosphorization (300 °C, 2 h, Ar), electrodeposition (−10 mA cm⁻², 10–1,800 s; −1.2 V, 50 C cm⁻²), calcination (300 °C, 1–2 h, Ar or 5% H₂), seawater (Yellow Sea), 1 M KOH, 1 M NaOH + seawater, 60–80 °C, LSV (10 mV s⁻¹), CV (2–100 mV s⁻¹), EIS (5 mV, 0.1 Hz–1 MHz), stability (1 A cm⁻²), SEM, XRD (10–90°, 5° min⁻¹), XPS (Al Kα, C 1s = 284.8 eV), HAADF-STEM (300 kV), TOF-SIMS (Cs⁺ sputter, Bi₃⁺ analysis), operando Raman (532 nm, −0.1 V vs RHE), ATR-SEIRAS (8 cm⁻¹, −1.02 to −1.52 V vs SCE)
