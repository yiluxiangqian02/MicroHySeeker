章节定位：Materials

关键实体：Nickel(II) nitrate hexahydrate, Iron(III) nitrate nonahydrate, Cobalt(II) nitrate hexahydrate, Chromium nitrate nonahydrate, Ammonium fluoride, Urea, Sodium monophosphate, Sodium hydroxide, Sodium chloride, Ethanol, Yellow Sea seawater, Deionized water
