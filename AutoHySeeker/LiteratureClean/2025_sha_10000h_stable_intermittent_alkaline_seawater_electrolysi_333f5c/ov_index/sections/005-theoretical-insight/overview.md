Section Positioning: This section provides a theoretical analysis of the oxidation behavior and protective mechanisms of the NiCoP–Cr₂O₃ catalyst under shutdown conditions, linking computational results with experimental observations.

Paragraph Index:  
P001: Describes adsorption energy calculations of OH⁻ on different metal sites to determine oxidation susceptibility, revealing Co oxidizes first, followed by P, while Ni remains protected; connects these findings to experimental data (TOF-SIMS, HAADF-STEM, XPS) and introduces multilayer structural models for further simulation.  
P002: Details simulations of oxygen diffusion across multilayer models, reporting low energy barriers for oxygen migration into CoPO₄ but high barriers beyond it, explaining how the CoPO₄ layer acts as a passivation barrier that shields Ni sites from deep oxidation.  
P003: Introduces computational models to evaluate how surface phosphate affects chloride ion (Cl⁻) adsorption, comparing bare and phosphate-coated surfaces of Ni, NiCoP, and NiCoP–Cr₂O₃.  
P004: Presents results showing that phosphate formation on NiCoP–Cr₂O₃ renders Cl⁻ adsorption on Ni sites energetically unfavorable (positive adsorption energy), suggesting a mechanism by which phosphate prevents corrosive Cl⁻ adsorption during shutdown.
