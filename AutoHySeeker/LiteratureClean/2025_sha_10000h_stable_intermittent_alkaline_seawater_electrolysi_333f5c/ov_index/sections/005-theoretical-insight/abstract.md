Section Role: This section presents theoretical insights into the oxidation behavior and protective mechanisms of NiCoP–Cr₂O₃ under shutdown conditions.  
Core Content: Density functional theory calculations reveal that Co exhibits the strongest OH⁻ adsorption (ΔG = −0.54 eV), followed by P (−0.31 eV) and Ni (−0.06 eV), leading to sequential oxidation forming CoO and CoPO₄ passivation layers; oxygen diffusion barriers increase from 0.31 eV (CoO→CoPO₄) to 3.41 eV (through CoPO₄) and 1.79 eV (CoO→NiCoP interface), while surface phosphate renders Cl⁻ adsorption on Ni energetically unfavorable (positive adsorption energy).
