Section Role: Abstract  
Core Content: This work introduces a gradient interlayer engineering strategy featuring Ni(11̄2)/Ni₃S₂(1̄20) heterointerfaces to create anti-reverse-current electrodes for alkaline water electrolyzers, achieving 1.79 V at 1000 mA cm⁻², >1500 h stability in 30 wt% KOH at 80°C, and resilience over 3600 accelerated startup/shutdown cycles.  
Key Entities: Ni(11̄2)/Ni₃S₂(1̄20) heterointerfaces, alkaline water electrolysis (AWE), 1000 mA cm⁻², 1.79 V, 30 wt% KOH, 80°C, >1500 h operational stability, 3600 startup/shutdown cycles, U.S. DOE 2026 target
