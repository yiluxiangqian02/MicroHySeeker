Paper Positioning: This paper introduces a gradient interlayer engineering strategy that creates heterointerface-enabled anti-reverse-current electrodes for alkaline water electrolyzers, achieving high activity, long-term stability, and exceptional resistance to reverse current degradation at industrially relevant current densities (1000 mA cm⁻²).
