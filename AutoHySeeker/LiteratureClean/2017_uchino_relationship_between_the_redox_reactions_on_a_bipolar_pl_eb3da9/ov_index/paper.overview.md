Paper Positioning: This paper investigates the mechanism of reverse current in bipolar-type alkaline water electrolyzers after shutdown, linking it to redox reactions on the bipolar plate and operational conditions.

Section Index:
  001-relationship-between-the-redox-reactions-on-a-bipolar-plate-and: Relationship Between the Redox Reactions on a Bipolar Plate and Reverse Current After Alkaline Water Electrolysis  
  002-introduction: Introduces the context of renewable energy integration with alkaline water electrolysis, highlights challenges posed by intermittent operation, and identifies the lack of mechanistic understanding of reverse current in bipolar electrolyzers.  
  003-experiment-method-and-analytical-model: Outlines the combined experimental and modeling approach used to study reverse current, including system setup and theoretical framework.  
  004-experiment-method: Details the physical configuration of the two-cell bipolar electrolyzer, electrode materials, operating conditions, measurement protocols for reverse current and cell voltages, and impedance-based resistance characterization.  
  005-potential-profile-model: Proposes a one-dimensional potential profile model for the bipolar electrolyzer after shutdown, defining key potentials, overpotentials, and deriving equations that relate cell voltages to the driving force for reverse current.  
  006-results-and-discussion: Presents experimental data showing how reverse current magnitude depends on prior current density, analyzes time-dependent cell voltage decay, identifies dominant redox couples, and validates the proposed model against measurements.  
  007-conclusions: Summarizes that reverse current arises from asymmetric redox states across the bipolar plate post-shutdown, driven primarily by NiO₂/NiOOH and H₂/H₂O couples, and ceases when both sides reach equilibrium oxidation states.

Key Entities: Nickel mesh electrodes, Nafion NRE212CS membranes, 7.0 M NaOH electrolyte, 25°C operating temperature, current densities (100–600 mA cm⁻²), reverse current measured via DC clamp meter, internal resistance (R_int) from AC impedance, manifold ionic resistance (R_m), cell voltages U₁ and U₂, redox couples [NiO₂/NiOOH] and [H₂/H₂O], open-circuit voltage decay, equivalent circuit model, potential profile equations.
