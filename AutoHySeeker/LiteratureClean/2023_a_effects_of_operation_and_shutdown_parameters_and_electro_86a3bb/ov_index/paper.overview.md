Paper Positioning: This paper investigates experimentally and theoretically how operational and shutdown conditions, as well as electrode materials, influence the reverse current phenomenon in alkaline water electrolyzers (AWE), which critically impacts catalyst durability under dynamic operation with renewable energy sources.

Section Index:
  001-effects-of-operation-and-shutdown-parameters-and-electrode-mater: Effects of operation and shutdown parameters and electrode materials on the reverse current phenomenon in alkaline water analyzers
  002-1-introduction: 1. Introduction – Contextualizes the role of AWE in renewable energy integration, identifies the reverse current phenomenon during shutdown as a key durability challenge, and outlines knowledge gaps regarding operating/shutdown parameters and electrode materials.
  003-2-experimental: 2. Experimental – Details the construction of a 4-cell bipolar plate AWE stack, including cell configuration, manifold design, instrumentation for measuring reverse current and electrode potentials, and the equivalent electrical circuit model used to interpret ionic current flow during shutdown.
  004-3-results-and-discussion: 3. Results and discussion – Presents experimental findings on how temperature, electrolyte circulation (with/without N₂ bubbling), and electrolysis current affect reverse current magnitude and electrode potential evolution; discusses the role of redox reactions during shutdown and spatial variation in reverse current across bipolar plates.
  005-4-conclusion: 4. Conclusion – Summarizes that reverse current is driven by post-shutdown redox reactions, is amplified by higher temperature and electrolyte flow during shutdown, and varies across stack positions; emphasizes implications for system design, protective strategies, and durability testing under dynamic operation.

Key Entities: Alkaline water electrolyzer (AWE) 4-cell bipolar stack, Zirfon™ PERL (UTP 500) separator, KOH electrolyte, reversible hydrogen electrode (RHE), DC milliampere clamp meters, potentiostat (BCS-815), electrolyte temperature (controlled via oil bath heat exchanger), electrolyte circulation rate (15 mL min⁻¹ per cell), N₂ bubbling during shutdown, oxygen evolution reaction (OER) and hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) catalysts, reverse charge, electrode potentials, ionic resistance of PTFE manifolds, emf of bipolar plates, cell voltage, gas impurity threshold (2 vol%), operating current density, shutdown duration, accelerated durability testing protocol.
