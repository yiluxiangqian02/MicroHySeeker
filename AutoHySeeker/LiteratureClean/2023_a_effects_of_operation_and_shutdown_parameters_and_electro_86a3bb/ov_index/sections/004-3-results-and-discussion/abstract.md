Section Role: This section presents the Results and Discussion of experimental and simulation studies on reverse current phenomena in alkaline water electrolyzers during shutdown.  
Core Content: It investigates the influence of electrode materials, dissolved gases, electrolyte circulation, operating temperature, and current density on reverse currents and electrode potential dynamics, linking these factors to electrocatalyst durability under intermittent operation.  
Key Entities: AN-1, AN-2, CA-1 electrocatalysts; bipolar plates (BP-1, BP-2, BP-3); KOH electrolyte (7 M); zero-gap single-cell and 4-cell stack electrolyzers; reverse current; electromotive force (emf); electrode potentials; COMSOL simulation; electrolyte circulation; shutdown conditions; dissolved gases (H₂, O₂, N₂ bubbling); operating temperature (30°C, 80°C); current density (0.4–1.0 A cm⁻²); equivalent resistance; cumulative reverse charge
